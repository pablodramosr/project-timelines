Key notes & dependencies

⚠ Both IRBs (UChicago and IEP) are currently under review. Approval of both is required before the program can start. A delay could push randomization and the Chat2Learn launch past the second week of June.

• Randomization is contingent on: (1) close of family registration, (2) digitization and cleaning of paper consent forms, and (3) IRB approval from both committees.

• WhatsApp audio tasks at endline are administered to a subsample of caregivers; phone surveys cover a broader sample.

• ECE Grade 2 data corresponds to the 2027 national standardized assessment. SIAGIE data will be collected continuously through 2030.

• The Chat2Learn program period is approximately 4.5 months (mid-June to end of October 2026).
